Spooky Word Hunt

Grab your flashlight and hunt for hidden words in a glow-in-the-dark Halloween word search made for kids and families. Drag a finger across the grid to find ghosts, pumpkins, witches and bats, and earn up to three stars on every puzzle.

  • Three age modes for ages 4 to 15, from a small 6x6 grid with picture hints to a big 11x11 grid.
  • 16 themed word packs, from Pumpkin Patch to Ghost Ship, with shareable result cards.
  • No ads, no accounts and no personal data collected. It works offline.
